Every Arcron keeper tick reads the beacon's revealed_round / revealed_seed globals and shelves the pair in the next slot of an 8-slot ring (one 320-byte box named ring, 8-byte round + 32-byte seed per slot). The board decodes the box straight from algod; the chain is the source of truth. Contract: smart_contracts/seed_cache/contract.py.

Unaudited. TestNet only. Not deployed. Read-only, no wallet.
